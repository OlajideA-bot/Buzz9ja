About Buzz9ja

Nigeria's Go-To Platform for Events & Experiences

Buzz9ja is Nigeria's go-to app for discovering events, securing tickets, and connecting with trusted vendors — all in one place. Whether you're looking for what's happening this weekend or planning ahead, Buzz9ja puts verified events and experiences right at your fingertips.

How It Works

Getting Started is Simple

Whether you're attending an event or hosting one, Buzz9ja makes the entire process seamless.

🎟️

For Attendees Discover & Book Events

1

Discover

Browse events happening near you or search by category, date, or location.

2

Book

Get your ticket or RSVP directly in the app with secure, instant confirmation.

3

Attend

Show up and enjoy — your ticket and access pass are right there on your phone.

🏪

For Vendors List & Manage Events

1

Get Verified

Complete NIN-based verification to become a trusted vendor on the platform.

2

List Your Event

Create and manage your own events on the platform with full control.

3

Manage

Track bookings and manage your listings from the vendor web portal.
